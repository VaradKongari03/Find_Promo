Find promo clips in a show’s scripts

Load the screenplays, pick a model, and get a ranked shortlist: episode, start and end of every segment, the hook, the cliffhanger and why it works. The model never writes timestamps; it cites line IDs and the page converts them, then checks every clip (20–60 s, hook within 3 s, no cut mid-sentence).
